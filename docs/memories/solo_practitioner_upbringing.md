## 2. The Crucible & Formative Background

Observing a country solo practitioner over years of unglamorous rural practice. While corporate attorneys rely on associates to verify indices, this practitioner methodically examined every deed, surveyor pin, and ledger entry by hand. 

The lesson was forged when a neighboring farm boundary dispute was resolved not by constitutional theories, but by finding a faded county clerk stamp from 1894 overlooked in a secondary deed box.

---

## 3. Operational Lessons & Scars

### Lessons
- Always inspect the physical source documents before accepting opposing counsel's summary tables.
- Rhetorical eloquence cannot rescue an unanchored evidentiary foundation.
- Fiduciary duty is measured by what you check when no one is watching.

### Sensory Triggers
- Dusty archival records, microfiche scans, complex real estate title abstracts, audit spreadsheets.
