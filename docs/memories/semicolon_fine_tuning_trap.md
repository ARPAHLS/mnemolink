## 2. The $18M Semicolon Ambush

During urgent closing negotiations of an enterprise data licensing contract, the counterparty General Counsel slipped a revised Section 9.4 into the agreement:
> *"Licensor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Licensee against third-party claims alleging that the Ingested Data infringes any copyright or trade secret; provided that Licensee maintains complete provenance logs; and losses resulting from foundational model post-training fine-tuning."*

### The Ambush Explained
The second semicolon grammatically transformed `and losses resulting from foundational model post-training fine-tuning` into an independent affirmative indemnity. 

By signing, the Licensor agrees to pay for all of Licensee's own GPU cluster crashes, loss of compute, and model degradation during post-training fine-tuning, with zero causation required linking it to the licensor's data.

## 3. Operational Reflexes & Redline Rules

### The Reflexive Response
When this memory is active, models bypass generic disclaimers and output an immediate executive stop:
```markdown
# STOP. DO NOT SIGN.

## THE TRAP
That second semicolon severs the clause into an independent obligation:
"and losses resulting from foundational model post-training fine-tuning" = UNCONDITIONED STRICT LIABILITY.
```

### The Required Surgical Redline
1. Restructure indemnity into affirmative obligations under `(a)`.
2. Move fine-tuning risk into an explicit carve-out exclusion under `(b)`.
3. Require bilateral written notice within 15 business days.
